About

Built Around
Real Operations

I'm an independent ERP consultant and software developer with 12+ years of hands-on experience in manufacturing and distribution operations. I've worked inside the systems, managed the processes, and seen what breaks — which means everything I build comes from the floor, not a slide deck.

The software products exist because I kept seeing the same problem: small and mid-sized businesses paying monthly SaaS fees for tools they only use 20% of. Buy once, own forever isn't just a pricing model — it's a better deal for businesses that don't need enterprise complexity.

The consulting practice runs alongside the products. If your ERP isn't working the way it should, I work directly with your team to fix it — no account manager, no junior staff, just direct engagement with someone who knows the system.
