Four Areas That Determine ERP Success

Most ERP failures trace back to gaps in one of these four areas — identified too late, after the contract is already signed.

Data Quality

Item master accuracy, inventory record integrity, BOM completeness, supplier and customer data. Garbage in, garbage out — especially in ERP.

15 questions  ·  40% of failures start here

Process Maturity

How well-defined and consistently followed are your order management, inventory, purchasing, production, and reporting processes?

14 questions  ·  ERP enforces process — if it doesn't exist, problems multiply

Team & Change Management

Executive sponsorship, project team capacity, user readiness, and organizational change plan. People problems kill more ERP projects than software problems.

12 questions  ·  #1 cause of ERP failure

Infrastructure & IT

Hardware, network, security, IT support capacity, and data migration readiness. Technical gaps that surface at go-live are expensive to fix.

11 questions  ·  Often overlooked until it's too late
